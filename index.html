RESEARCH INTERNSHIP · WPI

Wearable Respiratory Sensor

Miniaturizing a wearable sensing platform for oxygen, carbon dioxide, and hypoxia monitoring.

Working with PhD researchers under Professor Ulkuhan Guler, I designed a compact 6-layer sensor PCB in Altium, planned the component layout and layer stack, routed mixed analog and digital signals, and developed a penny-sized sensor head with 50+ components.
